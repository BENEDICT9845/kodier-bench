This prototype uses the data for evaluation. Before a **public or commercial** launch,
confirm each source's reuse terms:

- **G-BA (ANB)** — free; **derived products permitted** (notify G-BA), **raw XML must not be
  re-hosted** (we don't — only derived aggregates), redistribution must be **declared on the
  order form**, and attribution is mandatory (§ 4 Abs. 5–7). This repo complies: derived-only,
  attributed.
- **InEK** — DatenBrowser reuse terms are **not yet confirmed** and the related Begleitforschungs
  tool is non-commercial. **Confirm InEK's terms (or obtain a licence) before public/commercial
  use** of InEK-derived figures. Until then, keep deployment access-controlled.
- **BfArM / Destatis (DL-DE-2.0)** — reuse permitted with attribution.

## Privacy
No personal or patient data is processed. Values a user enters are computed in their browser
and are never transmitted or stored.
