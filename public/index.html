Where the gap is largest

Estimated annual revenue effect if your coded complexity matched the national average, holding your volume constant. Ranked high → low.

A gap is a signal to review, not proof of under-coding. Your case mix may genuinely be less severe than the national average — the tool points you where to look, it does not audit charts. Method: gap = national Ø cost weight − your Ø cost weight; € = gap × your cases × base rate. National reference from InEK §21 data (aG-DRG 2026); base rate is the Landesbasisfallwert you set.
